Overview

Bookkeeper is a Mac app that shows where your money goes. Drop in the CSV export from your bank and it draws your income and spending as rings you can open. Crypto holdings have their own side. Stocks are coming.

It runs on your Mac and nothing else. There is no account, no fee, no analytics, and it never connects to your bank. It is free, open source (GPL-3.0), and made by the House of Helios.

Who it is for

You want a clear picture of your money and would rather not hand a bank login to anyone.

  • Banking. You download a CSV from your bank's website and drop it on the window. Bookkeeper reads it on your Mac and shows income, spending and the total. Bank lines never leave the Mac. See Banking.
  • Crypto. You name a wallet and tell Bookkeeper what it holds. No wallet address is looked up and no key is asked for. Prices come through Tor from public sources. See Crypto.
  • Stocks is a door on the landing screen marked "Coming soon". Nothing works there yet. See Stocks.

What you need

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 14 or newer. Next: Install, then First run.
